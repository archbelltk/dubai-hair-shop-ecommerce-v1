Our Story

Quality & Luxury

Alicia Tally was born from a simple belief: African women deserve beauty brands that understand their hair, their skin, and their style, without compromise.

From our Dubai studio, we source the finest virgin human hair, craft bespoke fashion pieces that celebrate African heritage, and formulate skincare with botanicals that honor melanin-rich complexions.
